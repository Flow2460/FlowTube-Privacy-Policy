Privacy Policy for FlowTube

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FlowTube ("the Application"), developed by Florian Knecht ("the Author"), handles user information. The Author takes your privacy seriously and has designed the Application to respect your data.

1. No Personal Data Collection by the Developer

FlowTube does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage statistics, analytics, or telemetry with the Author or any developer-controlled servers. All user settings, configurations, and processed media files remain strictly local on your device.

2. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The Application requires an active internet connection to browse media, search for metadata, retrieve cover artwork, and process user-requested media streams. When you use these features, your device connects directly to external third-party platforms and APIs, specifically:

  • YouTube (Google LLC) – for browsing videos and retrieving user-requested audio streams. (Privacy Policy)
  • MusicBrainz (MetaBrainz Foundation) – for searching track, artist, and album metadata. (Privacy Policy)
  • Cover Art Archive (MetaBrainz Foundation / Internet Archive) – for retrieving cover artwork and license information. (Privacy Policy)

During these direct connections, technical data—such as your IP address, user-agent headers, and search queries—is transmitted directly from your device to the respective third-party servers to fulfill your request. The Author has no control over how these third-party services process your data. Please refer to the privacy policies linked above for details on how each service handles user data.

3. Local Storage and Browser Data

FlowTube uses an integrated browser session to allow you to navigate YouTube manually. Any cookies, cache files, or session data generated during browsing are stored strictly locally on your device. Additionally, FlowTube stores configuration files, temporary working files, and downloaded or edited media files exclusively on your local hard drive in the folders designated by you or the Windows operating system. You have full control over this data and can view, modify, or delete it at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 14–16 in the European Union).

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), Florian Knecht is the responsible party for this Application. Because no personal data is transmitted to or stored by the Author, we do not hold any personal data subject to access, correction, or erasure requests.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the Application or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
